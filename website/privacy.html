1. Who we are

PesaListas is a shared household planning app for families, couples, roommates, and small groups. It helps users organize groups, lists, tasks, chores, recipes, meal plans, shopping items, ideas, movies, activities, and voting-based decisions.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use PesaListas, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

Account and profile data Name, email address, profile picture/avatar, authentication identifiers, and display name.
Google Sign-In data Information provided by Google during sign-in, such as your email, name, profile image, and Google account identifier.
Groups and invitations Group names, descriptions, member information, invitation email addresses, invitation status, and role or membership information.
User content List names, descriptions, tasks, chores, deadlines, priorities, shopping items, recipes, ingredients, cooking instructions, meal plans, movies, ideas, activities, and votes.
Preferences Language, theme, notification settings, and other app preferences stored on your device or in your account.
Camera access If barcode scanning or similar features are enabled, camera access is used to scan codes. Camera access should be used only for the feature the user starts.
Notifications Notification permissions, device notification tokens, and message delivery information when reminders or push notifications are enabled.
Analytics and diagnostics App version, device model, operating system, crash traces, performance or usage events, installation identifiers, and technical logs.
Website data The static website does not intentionally collect form data or add tracking cookies. Hosting providers may process technical request logs such as IP address, browser, and request time.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To let you create, join, and collaborate inside shared groups.
  • To store, sync, and display lists, tasks, chores, recipes, meal plans, shopping items, votes, movies, ideas, and activities.
  • To send service-related notifications or reminders when you enable them.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability.
  • To understand basic app usage and improve the product.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

4. Legal bases for users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contract: to provide the app, account, authentication, collaboration, syncing, and core household planning features.
  • Consent: for optional permissions or features such as push notifications, and for analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the service, prevent misuse, fix bugs, understand reliability, and improve the app.
  • Legal obligation: when processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws or official requests.

5. Sharing and visibility

PesaListas is collaborative. Content you add to a shared group may be visible to other members of that group. For example, household members may see shared list items, chores, recipes, meal plans, shopping items, votes, and group membership details.

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in these situations:

  • With other members of the groups you join or create, as needed for collaboration.
  • With service providers that host, authenticate, store, analyze, debug, or deliver app functionality.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of the app, subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. International transfers

Some service providers may process or store data outside the country where you live, including outside the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as data processing agreements, standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions, or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

8. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to provide PesaListas, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and operate backups. Account data and user content are generally kept while your account is active or until deletion is requested, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Diagnostics and crash data may have shorter provider-specific retention periods. Deletion from backups and third-party systems may take additional time.

9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including secure authentication, provider security controls, and access limits. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account or personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, such as optional notifications.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

You can also manage some choices directly in the app, such as language, theme, notification settings, and permissions through your device settings.

11. Children

PesaListas is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal data without the required consent, contact us so we can review and delete the information where appropriate.
