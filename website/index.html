About the app

PesaListas is a collaborative household planning app built for families, couples, roommates, and small groups who want to stay organized together.

Features

  • 👥Shared groups with member invitations
  • ✅Tasks, chores, priorities, deadlines, and recurring work
  • 🛒Shopping lists with bought and to-buy sections
  • 🍳Recipes, ingredients, cooking instructions, and meal planning
  • 🗳️Voting for movies, ideas, and activities
  • 🌍English and Spanish interface with light and dark themes
